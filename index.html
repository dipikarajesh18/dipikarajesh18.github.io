ACM Creativity and Cognition 2026

with M Charity, Vera L. Zhong and Adam M. Smith

MVAuLT is a browser extension for authoring creative alternative text (alt text) for Music Videos (MV). While alt text is typically an informational description of an indi-vidual static image, we apply it to video through sequential MV screenshots for which users can batch-write interpretive alt textthat considers narrative context. We designed MVAuLT to mini-mize friction and maintain narrative coherence in alt text authoring. Through this work, we offer a novel consideration of alt text as both interpretive and informational, a new framework for multi-modalMV accessibility, and a lens of alt text as a participatory storytelling practice

MVAuLT: Authoring Interpretive Alt Text for Music Videos (2025)

ACM ASSETS 2025

with Vera L. Zhong, Ace S. Chen, Madison Gruender and Kathryn E. Ringland

Amorphous Interpretations is an evolutionary narrative generation system capable of interpreting events in an open-ended simulation to create thematically diverse and cohesive stories. We use the event log files from four human-designed environments from the abstract Amorphous Fortress engine as our domains to generate artificially “interpreted” stories. The raw event logs are sifted to extract significant narrative events that are then reduced to a grammar to systematically modify thematic elements of the story. By applying two different Quality-Diversity algorithms, and by restricting the modification and randomization of certain story elements, we discover that this system is capable of generating a wide range of stories from just a single event log.

Amorphous Interpretations: Diverse Narrative Generation for Open-Ended Simulation Environments (2025)

FDG INT Workshop 2025

with Julian Togelius and M Charity

This project presents One Spell Fits All, an AI-native game prototype where the player, acting as a witch, solves problems through magical conjurations. It also serves as a research platform for exploring AI-driven and AI-native game design, including AI-assisted creativity, player experience with predominantly AI-generated content, and the energy implications of local versus cloud-based models. The game uses compact, locally running LLMs and diffusion models to generate and evaluate content without external APIs or internet access, enabling a self-contained and responsive gameplay experience while supporting experimentation with LLMs in narrative game systems.

One Spell Fits All: A Generative AI Game as a Tool for Research in AI Creativity and Sustainable Design (2024)
